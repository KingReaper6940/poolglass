HOW THE COUNTS WORK

Two counts, kept separate.

First, which faces pass?

A single fair die has a known number of passing faces. That defines a binomial count of passing dice in the original pool.

Then, which dice stay?

If retention favors the passing end, retained successes are min(original successes, kept dice). Otherwise, some passing dice are discarded before the kept group is counted.

Finally, count the event.

Add exact ordered-outcome weights for “at least,” “exactly” or “at most” the required count. Rounded percentages are a display convenience, with exact fractions retained.

No simulations or random sampling are used. Outcome-mass checks catch conservation errors; they do not independently prove every probability. Small cases are separately tested by enumerating raw die faces, sorting and counting. No uploads, analytics or browser storage. Save before closing. Full model and limitations.
